## The project

Three ways of structuring historical sources so they can be questioned more effectively:

- **Relational databases**: building databases and writing SQL queries.
- **Mapping**: spatial analysis with QGIS, a geographic information system[^gis].
- **Text encoding**: XML/TEI[^tei] encoding of heritage documents.

[^gis]: A GIS links data to places, overlays it on maps (including old maps) and analyses it spatially.

[^tei]: The *Text Encoding Initiative* is the reference standard for encoding texts in XML: it describes the structure of a document as well as the people, places and dates it mentions.
