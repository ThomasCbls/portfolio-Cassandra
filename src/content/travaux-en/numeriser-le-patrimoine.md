## The project

Digitising a heritage object means both documenting it and making it accessible. This project allowed me to practise three techniques: object photography, RTI imaging[^rti] and, as an introduction, photogrammetry[^photogrammetry].

[^rti]: *Reflectance Transformation Imaging*: a series of photographs taken under different lighting angles lets you move the light on screen and reveal reliefs invisible to the naked eye (worn inscriptions, tool marks).

[^photogrammetry]: A technique that reconstructs a three-dimensional model from many photographs taken from different angles.
